He loved everything a typical 2-year old dog loves — car rides, playing with kiddos, long walks. But with Buckley Bumble, there was even more to love: a signature wobble. Buckley was born with cerebellar hypoplasia, (CH), a neurological condition that affects balance. It didn’t slow him down one bit — in fact, it seemed more like his special swagger.
